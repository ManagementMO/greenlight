## Scene

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: premium product-launch hero artwork for an evidence-grounded small-business cash decision tool. Landscape 16:9, high resolution, suitable for a GitHub README banner, Devpost cover, and a later motion-film keyframe.
Primary request: Create a striking, original editorial 3D still about making room for a business to grow while protecting payroll. One continuous broad mint-green ledger ribbon travels from the lower left into the right half of the frame, forms three precise horizontal terraces with vertical step changes like an event-time cash-flow line, and passes ABOVE a thin coral safety threshold into an architectural emerald-green open portal. The open space above the ribbon is the central visual idea: breathing room. Make the stepped ribbon unmistakably one connected path, not disconnected bars. The threshold is restrained, not a glowing laser. A faint secondary coral trajectory dips below the threshold before fading away, contrasting the two possible futures without any numbers or data claims.
Composition: polished art-directed wide composition. Main sculptural subject concentrated in the right 65 percent. Left 35 percent is intentional quiet negative space for future title placement, but do not put any text in this image. A low oblique orthographic camera, clean horizonless warm ivory studio ground. Precise geometry, subtle ledger-like ruled lines debossed into the ground and ribbon only, never a screen or a busy grid. Strong recognizable silhouette at thumbnail size.
Palette: warm ivory #F4F1E9, deep ink-green #103E34, clear mint #BDE8C4, restrained coral #D77862. Emerald portal with satin enamel, mint ribbon in tactile matte folded paper, softly brushed edges and realistic contact shadows. Warm directional daylight, beautiful subtle ambient occlusion, premium architectural model photography, calm confidence rather than futuristic spectacle.
Constraints: no people, no floating coins, no dollar symbols, no robots, no dashboard UI, no invented charts with axes or labels, no generic financial icons, no text, no lettering, no watermark, no purple or blue gradients, no plastic toy look. This is metaphorical brand artwork, not a screenshot or a measured financial chart. Carefully composed, memorable, simple enough to animate later.

Square canvas, a single centered solid dark ink-green #103E34 symbol with ample transparent margin. Make it a simple balanced typographic mark readable at 24px, two or three deliberate orthogonal forms with subtle small-radius corners. Flat vector-like finish, crisp contours, no effects. The useful visual idea is growth with breathing room. No text or wordmark, no coins, no arrows, no check mark, no chart axes, no gradients, no shadow, no 3D.
