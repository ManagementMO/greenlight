RESIDENTIAL RENOVATION AGREEMENT
Agreement No. BCH-2026-129 (Estimate est-00129)

Contractor: Birchfield Custom Homes LLC, 4417 Brickworks Road, Providence, RI
Owner: Harbor Lane Dental Group, LLC ("Owner")
Project: Interior renovation of the ground-floor clinic at 88 Harbor Lane, Providence, RI
Date: October 13, 2026

1. SCOPE OF WORK
Contractor will demolish the existing reception and two operatories, reframe the interior
walls, relocate plumbing and electrical for three new treatment rooms, and install new
cabinetry, flooring and finishes, as described in the drawings dated September 29, 2026.

2. SCHEDULE
Work begins October 19, 2026. Contractor will use reasonable efforts to reach substantial
completion by February 12, 2027, subject to permit timing and Owner-requested changes.

3. CONTRACT PRICE
The total contract price is $97,200.00 (ninety-seven thousand two hundred dollars).

4. PAYMENT SCHEDULE
(a) Deposit. A deposit of 15% of the contract price is due on signing, October 13, 2026.
(b) First progress payment. $29,160.00 becomes payable upon the rough-in inspection for
    framing, plumbing and electrical.
(c) Second progress payment. $29,160.00 is due on January 15, 2027, after drywall and
    finish carpentry are complete.
(d) Final payment. The final $24,300.00 is due on February 26, 2027, upon Owner's final
    acceptance of the work.

5. CONTRACTOR'S COSTS (for Owner's information)
Contractor has committed to the following purchases and subcontracts for this project:
  - Building permit, City of Providence: $1,000.00, due October 16, 2026
  - Framing lumber and materials, Ironbridge Supply: $18,500.00, due October 30, 2026
  - Framing subcontract, Novak Carpentry: $16,200.00, due November 20, 2026
  - Electrical and plumbing subcontract, Delmar Trades: $14,800.00, due December 18, 2026
  - Cabinetry and finishes, Coastline Millwork: $12,900.00, due January 29, 2027

6. CHANGE ORDERS
Any change to scope, price or schedule requires a written change order signed by both parties.

7. INSURANCE
Contractor maintains general liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence
and will provide a certificate of insurance to Owner before mobilization.

8. WARRANTY
Contractor warrants its workmanship for one (1) year from substantial completion.
